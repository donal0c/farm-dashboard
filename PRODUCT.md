# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

Facts below are drawn from the repository (README, docs/architecture.md,
CLAUDE.md, source adapters) and were not confirmed in an interview; treat each
as repository-evidenced rather than user-approved.

## Platform

web

## Users

An Irish farmer (dairy, beef, sheep, tillage or mixed) who opens the app once a
week, often on a phone, to decide what deserves attention in the coming days.
Secondary: a farm adviser checking the same evidence.

## Product Purpose

AgriView turns a small set of public Irish weather, land, water, scheme and
market sources into one weekly action, the checks around it, and the evidence
and uncertainty behind each call. Success is a farmer who can inspect the
source, scope, freshness and rule behind every statement and knows where the
app stops short of advice.

## Positioning

Every decision-bearing value exposes source, scope, freshness and confidence,
and the rules that rank priorities are deterministic, versioned and visible.
Nothing is generated by a language model. Missing data is shown as missing,
never as zero or sample values.

## Operating Context

- One saved manual farm pin (browser-local, Zustand) is the only location.
- Routing keys are approximate search aids, never exact Eircodes.
- Sources: Open-Meteo point forecast, Met Éireann warnings, DAFM LPIS 2024,
  DAFM nitrates layer, DAFM CAP 2025 county aggregates, EPA WFD 2019–2024,
  OPW water levels, CSO AEA01/AHM05, a hand-verified DAFM deadline list.
- Next.js route handlers are the ingestion and trust boundary.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Nearby, county and national evidence is never described as field-specific.
- Compliance copy prompts verification; it is not a legal determination.
- No accounts, database, analytics or advertising.
- Biodiversity data is deliberately omitted until a maintained source and a
  recurring farmer decision exist.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AgriView. Voice: plain, public-service, honest about limits.
- docs/design-lock.md pins the visual world: restrained field notebook,
  editorial hierarchy, Newsreader for display and IBM Plex Sans for data and
  controls, limestone paper light theme and desaturated night-field dark theme.

## Evidence on Hand

Live public data only; no testimonials, customers or benchmarks exist and none
may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The weekly brief is the primary workflow; everything else is evidence.
2. Deterministic rules before any AI layer.
3. Missing is not zero; unavailable is not sample data.
4. Scope and freshness travel with every value.
5. New public data needs a recurring farmer decision and a degraded-state design.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-operable workflows, 44 px targets, focus-trapped dialogs that restore
focus, WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both themes, reduced-motion respected.
